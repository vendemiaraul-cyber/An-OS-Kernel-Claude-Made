Playing on iPhone

No files needed

Quake, DOOM and Quake II ship as id Software’s original, unmodified shareware and demo archives. Your browser unpacks them when you tap Play!, so there is nothing to download or extract yourself.

Touch controls

Turn the phone sideways. The left stick moves, the right half of the screen looks around in Quake II, and the big red button fires. Bluetooth controllers and keyboards also work.

Saves stay put

Game saves and settings are stored in Safari on this phone. Quake II downloads 37 MB each visit, so use Wi-Fi.

Every game runs from this site. Engines and games keep their own open-source licences (GPL, MIT, BSD, MPL); Freedoom is BSD-licensed.
