Quake

The original 1996 shooter on WebQuake, a JavaScript port of the Quake engine. Plays the shareware episode, Dimension of the Doomed: 8 levels.

Unpacked here from id Software’s original 1996 shareware archive (8.7 MB download).

Touch: arrow buttons move, turn and strafe, Look ▲▼ aims, then Fire and Jump. Complete Level (Visual) turns on an autopilot that hunts the monsters and walks into the exit.


        
Engine: WebQuake by Triang3l, GPL-2.0 (licence). Shareware Quake is served as id Software’s unmodified quake106.zip.
