Quake II

Qwasm2, the Yamagi Quake II engine compiled to WebAssembly, runs right here.

Use Wi-Fi for the first download.

Touch: left stick moves, drag the right side to look, then Fire, Jump, Duck and Weapon. Keyboard, mouse and controllers work as on PC.


        
Engine: Qwasm2 (GPL-2.0), build from this repo. The demo is served as id Software’s unmodified q2-314-demo-x86.exe.
